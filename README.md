# Privacy Leakage in Social Networks

## 📖 Overview
This repository contains my research work on **Privacy Leakage in Social Networks**. It includes a detailed literature review and article summaries covering research published between **2020 and 2025**. The research explores privacy threats, information leakage, user behavior, legal frameworks, and modern privacy-preserving techniques used in social networking platforms.

## 📂 Repository Contents

- 📄 Literature Review
- 📄 Summary of 25 Research Articles (2020–2025)
- 📄 References

## 🎯 Research Objectives

- Study different types of privacy leakage in social networks.
- Analyze existing privacy threats and security challenges.
- Review modern privacy-preserving techniques.
- Identify research gaps and future research directions.

## 🔍 Topics Covered

- Privacy Leakage in Social Networks
- Privacy Threats and Security Risks
- User Behavior and Privacy Awareness
- Machine Learning and Privacy
- Differential Privacy
- Blockchain-based Privacy Protection
- Privacy-Preserving Image Sharing
- Legal and Ethical Issues
- Future Research Directions

## 📌 Key Outcomes
